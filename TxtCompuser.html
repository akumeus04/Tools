Syntax Guide

Write your music using space-separated tokens. The general format is Note:Duration.

Single Notes

Note name, optional accidental (# or b), and octave number.

C4:q D#4:e Bb3:w

Chords

Wrap comma-separated notes in square brackets.

[C4,E4,G4]:h

Rests

Use R to insert silence.

R:e R:q

Durations

  • w Whole (4)
  • h Half (2)
  • q Quarter (1)
  • e Eighth (0.5)
  • s Sixteenth (0.25)

Pro Tip

Newlines and extra spaces are ignored. Group your chords and melodies onto different lines to make your composition easier to read!
